- `2026-10-24-benchmarks.json`: Arena Text Overall (418 configurations, 10/22 source snapshot), CAI v2.0 configurations, archived Vals/benchmark references and methodology.
- `2026-10-24-western.json` and `2026-10-24-chinese.json`: official specifications, model cards, prices, subscription groups, events and unresolved evidence. They are reviewed inputs; `review-evidence.py` explicitly normalizes scope, variants, aliases and units.
- `2026-10-24-reviewed.json`: normalized decisions used by the generator. Beam parameter counts come from its official Architecture table; AA's billions-valued parameter fields are deliberately not auto-imported.
- `previous-2026-10-02.json`: complete previous constants retained for traceability. Old research/quotes remain accessible with historical labels.

## Remaining limits

1. Kimi K3.1 Pro release dates differ by source; its promised weights are not treated as already open. Its exact parameters are not borrowed from K3.
2. GLM-5.3 architecture versus tensor count remains unresolved. DeepSeek 1017 backbone, Engram and prefill/decode active counts are not merged into a single parameter value.
3. Hosted API aliases, regional pricing, long-input threshold boundaries, open-weight checkpoint identity and localized purchase amounts retain their evidence conditions or pending labels.
4. SciCode is under source review; scores stay visible but cannot receive a best-value highlight.
5. Arena efforts that differ from the row's AA configuration are explicitly marked and blocked from best-value calculation. Elo confidence intervals are retained; overlap prevents definitive winner highlights.
6. The official Terminal-Bench score table was not readable during collection. Displayed current model scores are AA's independent uniform-environment results; official and harness-specific outcomes are not mixed.
7. Older field checks and quote wording were not all reverified. They keep their original dates and historical status. No fabricated trends or ranking changes are generated.
